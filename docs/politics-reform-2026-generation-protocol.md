# R26-19 preparation and validation protocol

This protocol is fixed before inspecting fitted-model or stochastic diagnostics.
Use the modelling decisions for semantics and the preparation audit for measured
results. These thresholds test transfer adequacy for conditional synthetic
scenarios; they do not establish national forecast calibration.

## Source reconciliation

Pin original bytes and retain every input fingerprint. Reconcile 2022 list and
candidate totals at the old uninominal level before reconstructing municipal
attributions. Conserve each list's attributed college total exactly. Label
municipal reconstruction and special-contest ballot groups explicitly. Join
municipalities through the geographic catalog with ambiguity checks.

For preferences use recorded roster gender and person identity, never name-based
gender inference. Compare ministerial ER 2024 section aggregates with the later
regional municipal publication. Use the latter coherent release for fitting;
report differences without guessing their cause. Add national European 2024
municipal observations with gender/birth metadata. Preserve explicit zeros and
reject incomplete rosters or marks beyond source ballot budgets.

## Fitting and held-out validation

Fit effective intensity, length-adjusted concentration and local dispersion,
with equal election weights and partial pooling. Exclude each held-out province
or region and all observations involving its candidate identities from training.
Report cluster counts, marks/votes, leading-share, zero and tie diagnostics.
Predeclared transfer gates: mean absolute error at most 0.25 marks per vote and
0.25 leading-share units across folds, finite nonnegative fitted scales and no
invalid source budget. Exceedance blocks the default fitted profile; retain the
audit and require an explicit revised model decision rather than tuning a gate.

A single paired national election identifies historical contrasts, not future
error covariance. Shrink historical contrasts by half; future shock scale and
dependence are versioned assumptions. Older Senate history is age-confounded
(25+ versus Camera 18+) and is audited but excluded from central fitting.

## Stochastic and legal checks

Use seeds `r26-19-validation-0` through `r26-19-validation-999`, 1000 paired draws
for moment/normalization checks. Check marginal variance within 15% of its
declared budget before normalization, means within four Monte Carlo standard
errors, integer conservation and isolated random streams. Check weaker/central/
stronger dependence at correlations 0.5/0.85/0.98 and zero/doubled historical
gaps. Identical and independent votes are diagnostic benchmarks only.

Hold votes and candidatures fixed for preference sensitivity. Compare central,
concentrated/dispersed, weak/strong persistence, and suppressed/enhanced triple
preference profiles using 100 paired legal runs. Record filled/unfilled seats,
recoveries and candidate identity changes separately from vote-based entitlement.
Use the independently worked `completeScrutinyFixture(45)` for complete legal
propagation; reject a conditioning fixture with any non-computable stage or
fewer than 588 domestic entitlement entries.
Report Monte Carlo standard errors within each profile; profile spread is not
a confidence interval. Require realizable valid combinations, supplied-count
precedence, deterministic replay, and zero downstream representation-equivalence
discrepancies. Any legal stage reporting non-computability remains explicit.
