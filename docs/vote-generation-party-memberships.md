## Review assumptions

- This is a complete source-label proposal, not an approved official party genealogy. Opaque statistical continuities and core-party approximations are declared in notes.
- Articolo Uno and Possibile are separate because their 2019 memberships differ. Their later presence/absence and the extent of associated civic partners need review; do not add hidden equal-percentage allocations.
- Official result labels prove joint names, not every hidden party membership. Rows explicitly marked provisional need the cited campaign/history evidence refined before substantive calibration claims.
- First supported observation dates are support conventions, not inferred party founding dates. A cutoff-specific future split still requires cutoff-available distinguishing information.
- Never infer PATT, Green Italia, Libdem, AN or every small federation member as a separate parameter merely because its name appears in a logo or organizational history.
