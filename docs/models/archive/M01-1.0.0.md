# M01 1.0.0 — fixed compositions and component shocks

Status: implemented. M01 is the updated legacy-derived vote generator. M02 is
specified separately in [M02.md](M02-1.0.0.md). Both share geography, scenario controls,
reference resolution, physical-cell counting and legal scrutiny. Their probability
laws differ. This specification covers the corrected implementation, not archived
list-based legacy generators or the R simulator's original algorithms.

## Identity and semantic versioning

Canonical identity: **M01@1.0.0**. Scenarios store
`generation: { model: "M01", modelVersion: "1.0.0", ... }`; the registry is
[`model-registry.ts`](../../../web/src/lib/politics/reform-2026/vote-share/model-registry.ts).
M02 has an independent version sequence. The former current selector
`legacy-poll-informed-v1` identifies M01's lineage; it is not an accepted alias.
Historical receipts retain their original identifiers and do not acquire this version.
Opaque RNG namespaces, reference-row IDs and serialized preparation-field names
retain their existing spelling to preserve seeded behavior; they are not model selectors.

[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) is appropriate with an explicit public
contract: accepted inputs, denominators, probability law and output meanings.
Apply these rules independently to M01 and M02:

| Increment | Model-contract meaning |
| --- | --- |
| MAJOR | Change the law, assumptions, existing-input behavior or input/output meanings; changing a model-level default law also counts |
| MINOR | Add optional capabilities whose absence preserves every existing configuration's law and meanings |
| PATCH | Clarify the specification or correct an implementation to the same specified law without changing the contract |

A correction that changes the specified law needs MAJOR even if called a bug fix.
Released specifications are immutable; record a new version when their normative
content changes. Version 1.0.0 establishes this contract, not predictive calibration.
Exact replay additionally requires source/scenario IDs, fitted-state IDs, numerical
policy versions, RNG version, seed and implementation hash. A data refresh, code
refactor or file-schema revision is not automatically a new statistical model.
The browser currently accepts exact supported model versions, not SemVer ranges.

## Notation, observations and scope

- $t_*$ is the reference date; $T\ge t_*$ is election day. M01 elapsed time $D$
  is in days. $h\in\{C,S\}$ denotes Camera/Senato.
- $j=1,\ldots,K$ are stable political components; $j=0$ combines all electors
  who do not cast a valid list vote. It includes abstention, blank/invalid ballots
  and other residual non-list outcomes in the source contract.
- $c$ is a municipality, $r(c)$ its region, $i$ a physical ballot cell,
  $c(i)$ its municipality and $E_i$ its integer electorate.
- $p_{ij}$ is an elector-denominator probability, $\sum_{j=0}^Kp_{ij}=1$.
  Political reference shares are conditional on valid list voting; non-valid
  references are fractions of electors. Poll political percentages do not
  identify nonresponse or electoral turnout.
- $O_c$ is an original historical offered set, including 0; $F_i$ is the future
  cell's offered set, also including 0. Missing identity is an error, not absence.

Only cutoff-available sources are eligible. Historical preparation uses the
latest eligible same-date Camera/Senato pair with common age-18+ electorates;
there must be an eligible pair. M01 does not fit M02's historical state or
geographic uncertainty model. Its prepared geography stays fixed between draws.

## 1. Original component counts and conditional pooling

Let $y_{cg}$ be a historical list/group count with component domain $S_g$ and declared
nonnegative composition weights $w_{gj}$, including explicit zero weights:

$$
W_{gj}=\frac{w_{gj}}{\sum_{k\in S_g}w_{gk}},\qquad
x_{cj}=\sum_g y_{cg}W_{gj},\qquad
x_{c0}=N_c-\sum_{j>0}x_{cj}.
$$

Normally $S_g$ is the original/reviewed source membership. For an explicitly
opaque historical group only, a declared proxy may supply a different composition
domain, with provenance; this is an assumed proxy, not an observed party identity.
Proxies cannot invent unmeasured poll identities.

A singleton naturally has weight one. Joint groups require an explicit,
provenance-bearing composition; unresolved identities and missing compositions
fail. Canonical aliases may aggregate, but raw components may not be counted twice.
Fractional $x_{cj}$ are retained without rounding; original $y_{cg},N_c,O_c$ remain
available. Future ticket membership is binary, independently of $W$.

For usable rows in a donor area $A$, estimate latent support by conditional
frequency maximum likelihood:

$$
\widehat q_A\in\arg\max_{q\in\Delta^{K}}
\sum_{c\in A}\sum_{j\in O_c}x_{cj}
\log\frac{q_j}{q(O_c)},\qquad q(O_c)=\sum_{j\in O_c}q_j.
$$

Here $\Delta^{K}$ is the $K+1$-outcome simplex. The implemented MM iteration is

$$
q_j^{\rm new}\propto
\frac{\sum_cx_{cj}}{\sum_{c:j\in O_c}N_c/q(O_c)}
$$

on observed coordinates, normalized to their available mass. Initialize observed
coordinates proportionally to their total original counts, reserving donor mass
for unobserved coordinates. Unobserved regional coordinates retain national donor
mass. Convergence requires maximum coordinate
change below $10^{-12}$ within 10,000 iterations; failure is explicit.

Pool nationally first, then regionally. Regional evidence that cannot identify
relative masses between disconnected positive choice sets borrows those masses
from the national donor. A zero-probability alternative cannot identify a link
between otherwise disconnected positive sets. At national level such missing
relative information requires explicit current component references. Components
with no usable national support, and new components, likewise need references.
An identifiable regional zero is usable information, not a reason to seek positive
national support. There is no equal-list emergency fallback.

## 2. Local completion and observed-zero treatment

For local evidence, retain the donor mass of its original offered set and its
observed within-set proportions. With regional donor $d$ (national when no usable
regional evidence exists), the unregularized completion is

$$
q_{cj}=\begin{cases}
d(O_c)x_{cj}/N_c,&j\in O_c,\\
d_j,&j\notin O_c.
\end{cases}
$$

For logit-safe local preparation only, replace an **observed political zero** by
0.5 in the numerator of this expression, keeping the original $N_c$; then normalize
the completed vector. Positive fractions below 0.5 remain unchanged. Neither
original counts nor missing coordinates receive this substitution. Without a
usable local row, use the donor vector. Inferred support is a geographical
assumption, not an imputed historical observation or extra random error draw.

## 3. Poll measurement and reconciliation

Use eligible enabled Camera-context questions, reusing each observation across
chambers. Process questions by decreasing publication date, fieldwork end and
stable-ID tie order. Each component retains its latest contributing question;
apply retained older updates first. There is no separate poll-to-reference-date
movement step; future shocks start at $t_*$. Questions keep their original response set,
completeness, denominator and group compositions. The original dated Other answer
is its offered complement, not an observation of a later new party.

For reported group share $g$, effective sample $n$ and simulation $s$:

$$
\sigma_g=\sqrt{\left[\frac{\sqrt{g(1-g)/n}}{\max(0.01,g(1-g))}\right]^2+0.08^2},
\qquad \widetilde g=\operatorname{logistic}(\operatorname{logit}g+\sigma_gZ_{sg}).
$$

Endpoints remain exact. $Z_{sg}$ is one group measurement draw, shared across its
composition members, all geography and both chambers. The central preparation uses
$Z=0$. For a complete question set $B=0$; otherwise $B=1-\sum_g g$ is an unreported
residual, with no invented composition or independent measurement-error draw:

$$
G_{sg}=\frac{\widetilde g}{B+\sum_h\widetilde g_h},\qquad
R_{sj}=\sum_g W_{gj}G_{sg}.
$$

Complete questions must centrally sum to one; partial totals must not exceed one.
A partial question naming an exhaustive basis but leaving unexplained residual
mass fails. For a complete smaller offered set $O$, reconcile $R_{sj}$ conditional
on $O$, retaining each cell's probability outside $O$ and its non-valid support.
For partial questions, reconcile named shares against the full political basis,
retaining the residual. Raking uses elector-weighted valid support over ordinary
physical colleges; it never splits national poll shares across territorial ticket
labels. Components inherit their group's error; they do not get independent copies.

## 4. Scenario references at the reference date

References target components, including components in joint tickets. Resolve
national, region, province, municipality in that order; later specific scopes
supersede broader targets on their cells. At each scope an explicit chamber
reference overrides the common-derived reference for that chamber.

**M01 ignores scenario-reference SDs**, political and non-valid alike. Their means
are fixed reference-date support; original-poll measurement error above remains.
For a common anchor $r$ and compatible central chamber political shares $b_C,b_S$:

$$
\delta=\tfrac12g\operatorname{clip}_{[-1,1]}
 [\operatorname{logit}b_S-\operatorname{logit}b_C],\qquad
r_C=\operatorname{logistic}(\operatorname{logit}r-\delta/2),\quad
r_S=\operatorname{logistic}(\operatorname{logit}r+\delta/2).
$$

$g$ is the scenario chamber-gap sensitivity; endpoint anchors stay exact. Means
refer to the common anchor before this transformation. Unsupported contrasts have
labelled fallbacks. Explicit chamber references replace derived values.

For political targets in a scope, hold $a_i=p_{i0}$ fixed and multiplicatively
rake supported component/group scores to the requested aggregates:

$$
p'_{ij}=(1-a_i)\frac{b_{ij}e^{\lambda_j}}{\sum_{k>0}b_{ik}e^{\lambda_k}},
\qquad
\frac{\sum_i E_i p'_{ij}}{\sum_iE_i(1-a_i)}=r_j.
$$

Untargeted components form a residual category preserving within-cell ratios.
Only original support or an explicitly declared missing/new support policy may
seed an unsupported positive target. For a non-valid target, rake the two
categories with elector weights and preserve each cell's political ratios;
interior solutions have $a'_i=\operatorname{logistic}(\operatorname{logit}a_i+\lambda)$.
An explicit non-valid reference itself permits labelled support at a previously
zero category; the implementation uses 0.5 starting weights at such endpoints.

Validate identities, dates, compatible distributions, scopes and mandatory
reference coverage in every applicable filed cell. Do not constrain these
reference-date aggregates by future filing capacity. No earlier national margin
is reimposed after a specific override or future movement.

## 5. Future component movement

Let $P_j=\sum_i E_i p^*_{ij}/\sum_iE_i$ be unrestricted reference support after
polls and scenario interventions, including $j=0$. With retained scenario/context
scale settings $\sigma_N,\sigma_R,\sigma_L$ per square-root day
(defaults 0.02, 0.01, 0.01; the central dependence profile has $v=0.5$,
$r=0.7$, $g=1$, hence $\rho=0.85$):

$$
D=\operatorname{days}(T)-\operatorname{days}(t_*),\qquad
P^T_j=\operatorname{logistic}(\operatorname{logit}P_j+\sigma_N\sqrt D\,Z_{Nj}),
$$

$$
u_{ij}=\operatorname{logistic}\left[
\operatorname{logit}P^T_j+\operatorname{logit}p^*_{ij}-\operatorname{logit}P_j
+\sqrt D(\sigma_RZ_{r(i)j}+\sigma_LZ_{c(i)j})\right],\qquad
p^T_{ij}=u_{ij}/\sum_k u_{ik}.
$$

Local endpoints 0/1 remain fixed in the corresponding coordinate before
normalization. All future terms start at $t_*$, so $D=0$ adds no future movement.
Innovations are independent between components and between national/regional/local
streams. A component's national draw is shared nationally, regional draw within
its region and local draw across its municipality's physical splits.
For each corresponding Camera/Senato Gaussian pair,

$$
Z_S=\rho Z_C+\sqrt{1-\rho^2}\,\epsilon_S,\qquad
\rho=v+(1-v)r,
$$

where $v$ and $r$ are the declared shared-variance and residual-correlation profile
settings. No additional selected-model geographic estimation errors are sampled.
Logit transformation, normalization, shared measurements/references and count
conservation induce output dependence and can change expectations. M01 does not
claim unbiased logistic-normal means or unchanged old list-level uncertainty.

## 6. Future ballot conditioning, counts and list attribution

Only after movement apply deterministic proportional conditioning:

$$
\pi_{ij}=\frac{\mathbf1\{j\in F_i\}p^T_{ij}}{\sum_{k\in F_i}p^T_{ik}},\qquad
(X_{i0},\ldots,X_{iK})\sim\operatorname{Multinomial}(E_i,\pi_i).
$$

The denominator must be positive. Missing/non-finite support or an empty remaining
choice set fails explicitly. Conditioning has no additional substitution draw.
It reallocates removed mass among political and non-valid alternatives; historical
valid-vote totals and pre-filing national targets are not restored.

For future list $\ell$, binary membership $A_{i\ell j}$ identifies its components:

$$
V_{i\ell}=\sum_{j>0}A_{i\ell j}X_{ij},\qquad
V_{b\ell}=\sum_{i\in b}V_{i\ell},\qquad
X_{i0}+\sum_\ell V_{i\ell}=E_i.
$$

One component cannot compete in two lists on one ballot. Different nonoverlapping
territorial labels can carry the same component. Counts are generated in their
actual destination cells; there is no municipal greedy relocation or restoration
of reference margins. The Camera TAA proportional view is derived once from its
physical special ballots. Supplied votes/preferences retain the shared legal
assembly contract. Other is an explicit residual list without seats/candidates.

## Defaults, audit and limits

The checked-in default policy holds matched 2022 common-18+ municipal electorates
fixed for both future chambers. Its compositions are explicit assumptions. The
six missing national anchors (IdV, Futuro Nazionale, PLD/LibDem, +Uno, Progetto
Civico, PSI/civici) use means from 256 deterministic **M02 reference-only draws**
before adding those anchors. M01 fixes those means; M02 retains their sample SDs.
These are model-based scenario inputs, not new observations. Scoped overrides
retain precedence. They are regenerated, not supplied by a runtime fallback.

Record the model/version, source and implementation identities, original-count
provenance, pooling/compositions, fallback/unsupported coordinates, reference-date
support, election-date support before filing, filed support and realized counts.
Fixed references do not fix election-day counts. Geographic borrowing,
compositions, shock scales and chamber dependence are assumptions whose predictive
calibration remains separate from numerical/functional acceptance.
