# M02 3.0.0 — shared political field and bounded transfers

M02@3.0.0 replaces M02@2.0.0. It implements the
[M02-shared candidate](../../research/vote-generation/campaign-2026-11/models.md#m02-shared-research-v1)
on the complete component basis, with physical filings and explicit references.
The changed Senate probability law requires a MAJOR version. M01 remains 2.0.0.
Numerical policies, sources, fitted states and implementation hashes have separate
identities. The [2.0.0 specification](M02-2.0.0.md) retains the superseded law;
it is not an active selector or replay kernel.

Use [model acceptance](../model-acceptance-2026-11.md) for receipts and limitations,
and [common execution](../common-politics-scenario/execution.md) for workers,
legal assembly and cache boundaries.

## Unchanged statistical inputs

Grouped national information fitting, Camera/European context separation,
posterior diagnostics, empirical profiles and reference-family calibration remain
as specified in sections 1–3 and 6 of [2.0.0](M02-2.0.0.md). Historical joint
groups remain joint observations. Missing local history is not an observed zero.
New components require explicit reference support and geographic/innovation
fallbacks. Fractional M01 compositions never become M02 component observations.

Defaults remain `bounded-mass-pair-field-v2`, forecast scale 1.5, regional
reference-profile variance share 0.25 and conditional reference normalization.
Existing optional submethods operate on the one common field. No fitted
parameter is added.

## One unrestricted municipal reference field

Let $c$ index municipalities, $j=1,\ldots,K$ political components and $j=0$
non-valid support. Select **one** Camera political posterior row and one Camera
participation row. One Camera geographic-profile realization supplies

$$
p_{cj}\propto b_j\exp(m_{cj}+s_{cj}\xi_{cj}),\qquad
b=(a,(1-a)q),\qquad
\xi_{cj}=\sqrt{\rho_R}Z_{r(c)j}+\sqrt{1-\rho_R}Z_{cj}.
$$

All declared components participate before future filing conditioning. There is
one innovation address per municipality, independent of either law's physical
subdivision. Optional country calibration acts on unrestricted support. There is
no separate Senate posterior row, Senate profile noise or Senate movement path.
The chamber profile's gap multiplier still controls its descriptive contrast.
Its old copula correlation settings create no residual Senate latent shock.

## Descriptive contrast and references

Retain the cutoff-eligible common-18+ paired adapter: clipped national political
log ratios $d_j$, non-valid log-odds gap $d_0$ and shrunk municipal residual
profile differences $b_{cj}$. Missing contrasts use its labelled zero gap; the
adapter's additional Senate profile-SD budget is not sampled. For sensitivity $g$:

$$
a_{Sc}=\operatorname{logistic}(\operatorname{logit}a_c+g(d_0+b_{c0})),\qquad
q_{Scj}=\frac{q_{cj}\exp(g(d_j+b_{cj}))}{\sum_kq_{ck}\exp(g(d_k+b_{ck}))}.
$$

Apply sampled common references and explicit chamber references to these
**unrestricted** reference surfaces, using the existing common-anchor rule and
national → region → province → municipality precedence. An explicit chamber
target replaces that chamber's common value. Political interventions retain
turnout; non-valid interventions retain political proportions. Reference SDs and
normalization keep the calibrated-family rules. A pre-filing reference is not a
hard post-filing territorial margin. Traces and legal bonus support report
effective, conditioned central probabilities.

Call the resulting reference surfaces $r_C,r_S$. The common movement anchor is
$r_C$. For Senate-only renewal, pull $r_S$ back through the paired descriptive
contrast before movement. If a joint renewal explicitly introduces positive
Senate support where Camera has zero, seed that coordinate from the Senate
reference and renormalize the common anchor's political mass. The final transport
restores Camera's zero. An all-non-valid Camera anchor can similarly borrow
positive Senate support. This is a declared forecast-support rule, not a
historical split. No epsilon supplies missing support.

Each component has one national and one local innovation multiplier shared by
both chambers. Absent/null values inherit 1. Conflicting carrying-list budgets
fail with `COMMON_FIELD_MULTIPLIER_CONFLICT`; the editor writes both chamber
rows together. Reference means and SDs can still intentionally differ.

## One movement, then fixed reference transport

Apply the selected bounded movement and election-discrepancy kernel **once** to
the common anchor, giving $f$. Equations and scale estimation remain those of
section 7 of [2.0.0](M02-2.0.0.md), now on one unrestricted municipal field.
Whole municipal conditional Camera electorates weight aggregate mass priorities.
New components retain their declared innovation scales. Zero calendar horizon
still includes election discrepancy.

Transport $f$ to chamber $h$ using the reference differences between $r_h$ and
the common anchor $r$. For positive coordinates:

$$
a^F_h=\operatorname{logistic}(\operatorname{logit}f_0+
\operatorname{logit}r_{h0}-\operatorname{logit}r_0),\qquad
q^F_{hj}\propto\frac{f_j}{1-f_0}
\frac{r_{hj}/(1-r_{h0})}{r_j/(1-r_0)}.
$$

Explicit endpoint zeros/ones remain exact. Positive transported components must
already exist in the common movement support. Different chamber references remain
different. Equal references and zero gap produce equal latent forecast fields,
including at long horizons.

## Physical ballots and scrutiny

For physical cell $i$ in chamber $h$, let $F_{hi}$ contain outcome 0 and filed
components. Condition after common movement and chamber transport:

$$
P_{hij}=\frac{\mathbf1_{j\in F_{hi}}f_{hc(i)j}}
{\sum_{k\in F_{hi}}f_{hc(i)k}},\qquad
N_{hi}\mid P_{hi}\sim\operatorname{Multinomial}(E_{hi},P_{hi}).
$$

The same conditioning defines central reference metadata and bonus support.
Conditional counts are independent across chambers and physical cells, use their
own integer electorates, and conserve every elector. Joint lists sum component
counts. Votes are not relocated to repair a filing. The derived TAA proportional
view sums actual ballots and adds no draw. Rosatellum and reform consume these
counts through their unchanged legal assembly and scrutiny contracts.

## Forecast limitation

Paired and reference-induced contrasts remain fixed through future movement.
One same-age paired election cannot estimate temporal contrast innovations or
certify future signed-difference coverage and joint-bonus probabilities. The
research reconstruction is not held-out forecast validation. Inspect gap
sensitivities 0/1/2 while retaining explicit chamber references. Operational
acceptance does not remove this limitation.
