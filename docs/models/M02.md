# M02 1.0.0 — joint reference estimation and bounded spatial transfers

Status: implemented; default vote generator. M02 is the updated model formerly
called the new/selected model (`selected`). Its current identity is **M02@1.0.0**,
stored as `generation: { model: "M02", modelVersion: "1.0.0", ... }`.
[M01](M01.md) has a separate probability law and version sequence.

The [model-contract SemVer rules](M01.md#identity-and-semantic-versioning) apply:
a change to this law or the meaning of an existing configuration needs MAJOR;
optional compatible extensions use MINOR; law-preserving clarifications/fixes
use PATCH. Version 1.0.0 establishes the first named contract, not predictive
calibration. Data, numerical algorithms, file schemas, RNG and implementation
hashes remain separately identified. Scenario fields such as `models.statistical`
and `models.forecast` name submethods; `generation.model/modelVersion` identifies
the complete law. Historical research candidates and receipts
are not retroactively named M02 1.0.0. Only exact supported versions execute.

## Notation and pipeline

$t_*$ is the cutoff/reference date, $T$ election day, $\Delta=(T-t_*)/365.25$
in years. $j=1,\ldots,K$ denotes political components and $j=0$ all electors not
casting a valid list vote. $c$ denotes municipalities, $r(c)$ regions and $i$
physical ballot cells with integer electorates $E_i$. Historical fitting contexts
are Camera ($C$) and European ($E$); future chambers are Camera and Senato ($S$).
European posterior coordinates are never relabelled Senate.

M02 performs: original grouped observations → reference fit → empirical geography
and paired Senate adaptation → unrestricted reference-date calibration → future
bounded movement and discrepancy → local filing conditioning → cell multinomial
counts → future-list aggregation → shared legal assembly. Future filing does not
constrain reference-date support or restore earlier margins after movement.

## 1. Original observations and identification

Every eligible historical stratum retains its original offered political set,
group counts, electorate, non-valid count, context and date. Strata from one
historical event are not independent replications of historical movement.
Polls retain original reported groups, effective political sample and shared
survey-error group. A dated Other answer is its original offered complement.
M01 composition weights are never M02 component observations. M01's supplemental
partial named observations are not complete survey inputs for M02.

For record $a$, let $S_a$ be its binary group/component incidence matrix,
$u_a=\mathbf1^\top S_a$ its offered mask, and $r_a$ its observed political group
shares. For valid-political support $q_k$ in context $k$:

$$
\pi_{ag}(q_k)=\frac{S_{ag}q_k}{u_aq_k},\qquad
A_a=H_a(S_a-r_a u_a),\qquad
H_a\mathbf1=0,\quad H_aH_a^\top=I.
$$

$H_a$ is a Helmert contrast matrix. $r_au_a$ is an outer product. Historical zeros stay zero in $r_a$; absent choices are omitted from $u_a$, not
observed zeros. The supported poll-preparation path rejects printed-zero political
answers rather than inventing positive measurements.
Stack $x=(q_C^\top,q_E^\top)^\top$ and the context-block designs $X_a$.
The information approximation is

$$
0\mid x,\widehat C\sim\mathcal N(Xx,\widehat C),\qquad
L(x)\propto\exp[-\tfrac12(Xx)^\top\widehat C^{-1}(Xx)].
$$

This is a Gaussian share-residual approximation, not an exact grouped-count
multinomial historical-state posterior.

Components with identical columns across all original eligible partitions fit
as one identifiable group total. The following equations then operate on that
quotient basis: in §2, $K$ is its dimension; later indices return to the original
component basis. The stored equal division back into component coordinates is
only an array representation, not an estimated internal split. Other overlapping
unidentified directions retain their original operators and are detected by
linear-rank/nullspace checks. Required explicit component references supply
unidentified current contrasts; a proper regularization penalty is not evidence
that those contrasts were observed.

## 2. Covariance, scales and bounded reference posterior

For record sample size $n_a$, use original shares for the residual but half-count
variance shares for historical elections:

$$
\widetilde r_{ag}=\frac{y_{ag}+0.5}{n_a+0.5G_a}\quad\text{(election)},\qquad
\widetilde r_a=r_a\quad\text{(poll)},
$$

$$
B_a=H_a\left[\frac{\operatorname{diag}(\widetilde r_a)-\widetilde r_a\widetilde r_a^\top}{n_a}
+\frac{\omega_a^2}{12}I\right]H_a^\top,
\qquad \omega_a=0\text{ (elections)},\ 0.001\text{ (polls)}.
$$

Poll $n_a$ is the upstream effective question sample, including the declared
design-effect/decided-response policy. The half-counts above change covariance,
not historical observations. At linearization $\bar q$, let

$$
m_a=\max(10^{-5},u_a\bar q),\quad
J_{\rm survey}=\operatorname{diag}(\bar q)-\bar q\bar q^\top,\quad
s_j=\max(\bar q_j,h_j),\quad
J_{\rm move}=\operatorname{diag}(s)-\bar q s^\top.
$$

$h_j$ is the largest separately observed share of $j$, otherwise the smallest
observed group share containing it, with floor $10^{-4}$. If
$\tau_a=\max(0,t_*-t_a)$ and $\ell(a)$ is a poll's declared survey-error period/context:

$$
\widehat C_{ab}=
\widehat\sigma^2\min(\tau_a,\tau_b)A_aJ_{\rm move}J_{\rm move}^\top A_b^\top
+0.12^2\mathbf1_{\ell(a)=\ell(b)\text{ for polls}}A_aJ_{\rm survey}J_{\rm survey}^\top A_b^\top
+\mathbf1_{a=b}m_a^2B_a+10^{-16}\mathbf1_{a=b}I.
$$

All ages here are years. Movement errors are shared across the fitting contexts.
The last tiny diagonal is the specified arithmetic-noise convention, not a new
statistical prior. Bounded whitening/orthogonal Gaussian elimination computes
the same information matrix for heterogeneous strata without an extra ridge;
matrix-free covariance solving remains available within the workspace policy.

For consecutive same-kind historical elections $e,f$, use separately observed
components with shares at least 0.005 at both dates. With half-count-smoothed
shares $z$ and duration $d_{ef}$:

$$
R_{ef}=\operatorname{median}_{j<k}
\frac{[\log(z_{fj}/z_{fk})-\log(z_{ej}/z_{ek})]^2}{2d_{ef}},\qquad
\widehat\sigma=\operatorname{clip}_{[0.12,0.70]}
\sqrt{\frac{4(0.35)^2+\sum R_{ef}}{4+M}}.
$$

For observed non-valid fractions $a_e$:

$$
R^0_{ef}=\frac{[\operatorname{logit}a_f-\operatorname{logit}a_e]^2}{d_{ef}},\qquad
\widehat\sigma_0=\operatorname{clip}_{[0.05,0.50]}
\sqrt{\frac{4(0.15)^2+\sum R^0_{ef}}{4+M_0}}.
$$

The four regularization units and floors are fixed conventions, not invented
elections. The 0.005 threshold affects scale estimation only.

With $u_K=\mathbf1/K$, $D=(-I,I)$ and
$W=\operatorname{diag}[(0.5s_j)^{-2}]$, the political target is

$$
Q=X^\top\widehat C^{-1}X+4I+D^\top WD,\quad
b=4(u_K^\top,u_K^\top)^\top,\quad
p(x\mid\mathcal D)\propto e^{-x^\top Qx/2+b^\top x}
\prod_{k=C,E}\mathbf1\{q_k\ge0,\ \mathbf1^\top q_k=1\}.
$$

Exactly three feasible GLS updates construct covariance at $\bar q$, find the
simplex-constrained mode, then update $\bar q$ from the context-mode average with
$10^{-4}$ initialization floor and renormalization. The third target is frozen
for sampling; covariance hyperparameter uncertainty is not integrated out.
Initialization walks smoothed historical group totals, preserving existing
within-group proportions and unsupported coordinates; it creates no observations.

Non-valid support has its own independent reference fit. Put $v_k=(a_k,1-a_k)$,
$p_e=N_{e0}/N_e$ and $\bar a$ the latest training non-valid fraction. Residual
$[(1-p_e),-p_e]v_k=a_k-p_e$ has covariance

$$
C^0_{ef}=\widehat\sigma_0^2\bar a^2(1-\bar a)^2\min(\tau_e,\tau_f)
+\mathbf1_{e=f}\{p_e(1-p_e)/N_e+10^{-16}\}.
$$

Use simplex center $(0.5,0.5)$, precision 4 and a context-difference penalty
with scale $0.5\bar a(1-\bar a)$ on both coordinates. Political and non-valid
posteriors are independent before their later multiplication/normalization.

For either Gaussian target, an admissible transfer direction $d$ gives

$$
z\mid x\sim\mathcal N\left(
\frac{d^\top(b-Qx)}{d^\top Qd},\frac1{d^\top Qd}\right)
\quad\text{restricted to }x+zd\ge0.
$$

Four seeded chains preserve each simplex sum. Rank-normalized split/folded
R-hat must be at most 1.05, bulk ESS at least 400 and tail ESS at least 200.
Retained draws extend from 2,000 to 4,000/8,000 per chain when needed. Numerical
convergence is separate from predictive calibration.

## 3. Empirical geographic profiles

For each original local offered set $O$, let $m_e(O)$ be the mass of compatible
national observable groups fully contained in $O$. Use
$z_{eg\mid O}=z_{eg}/m_e(O)$ when that exact group baseline exists. Otherwise
use the original same-offered-set stratum's group count/valid exposure with
half-count smoothing. If no matching baseline is usable, skip that feature;
do not fabricate component observations. The conditional non-valid baseline is
$a_{e\mid O}=a_e/[a_e+(1-a_e)m_e(O)]$ when $m_e(O)>0$, otherwise $a_e$.

For local electorates $N_{ce}$, valid votes $V_{ce}$ and $G_{ce}$ reported groups:

$$
f_{ce0}=\operatorname{logit}\frac{N_{ce0}+0.5}{N_{ce}+1}
-\operatorname{logit}a_{e\mid O},\quad
R_{ce0}=\frac1{N_{ce0}+0.5}+\frac1{V_{ce}+0.5},
$$

$$
f_{ceg}=\log\frac{(y_{ceg}+0.5)/(V_{ce}+0.5G_{ce})}{z_{eg\mid O}},
\qquad R_{ceg}=\frac1{y_{ceg}+0.5}.
$$

Logarithm baselines have a $10^{-10}$ arithmetic floor. Prefer a component's
separate local history; otherwise its most recent observed joint-group feature;
otherwise regional history, with national zero log-profile center when no usable
regional feature exists. Observed zeros remain usable features. Missingness is
labelled, not an observed zero or implicit positive component allocation.

Initialize with time/kind-weighted features and shrink to regional centers:

$$
w_e=2^{-\operatorname{age}(e)/5}(2\mathbf1_{k(e)=C}+\mathbf1_{k(e)\ne C}),\quad
\bar f_{cj}=\frac{\sum w_ef_{cej}}{\sum w_e},\quad
\gamma_{rj}=\frac{\sum_{c\in r}\sqrt{I_{cj}+1}\bar f_{cj}}{\sum_{c\in r}\sqrt{I_{cj}+1}},
$$

$$
m^{\rm init}_{cj}=\frac{I_{cj}}{I_{cj}+500}\bar f_{cj}
+\frac{500}{I_{cj}+500}\gamma_{r(c)j}.
$$

$I$ is mean exposure: electors for outcome 0 and valid votes for political
features. Missing local histories use the regional center; absent regional
histories use center zero. Region membership uses the latest historical row
or target metadata if missing.

Across consecutive chronological elections, including cross-kind transitions,
use separately observed feature changes in at least three municipalities:

$$
L_{ef,j}=\max\left(0,
\frac{\operatorname{Var}_c(f_{cfj}-f_{cej})-operatorname{Mean}_c(R_{cej}+R_{cfj})}{d_{ef}}\right),
$$

$$
L_*=\operatorname{clip}_{[0.03^2,0.8^2]}\operatorname{median}_{ef,j}L_{ef,j},\quad
(\tau_j^{\rm fit})^2=\operatorname{clip}_{[0.03^2,0.8^2]}
\frac{4L_*+\sum_{ef}L_{ef,j}}{4+M_j}.
$$

Use $L_*=0.25^2$ if no statistic exists. Initialize profile variance $P=1$;
process available features chronologically, with the first prior variance 1:

$$
P^-=P+(\tau_j^{\rm fit})^2d,\quad
\mathcal R=R+0.15^2\mathbf1_{k(e)\ne C},\quad
G=P^-/(P^-+\mathcal R),\quad
m\leftarrow m+G(f-m),\quad P\leftarrow(1-G)P^-.
$$

Propagate after the last observation to $t_*$. Return

$$
d_{cj}=m,\qquad s_{cj}^2=P+
\begin{cases}0.10^2,&\text{separate history},\\0.45^2,&\text{pooled/missing history}.\end{cases}
$$

Without local entries keep the fallback mean and $P=1$. Future local innovation
SD is the common $\sqrt{L_*}$ for all fitted outcomes, while profile filtering
uses the component-specific $\tau_j^{\rm fit}$. These are empirical-Bayes features
with reused historical information, not an independent second likelihood.

## 4. Senate reference and chamber dependence

Use a cutoff-eligible, same-electorate, age-18+ paired Camera/Senato election.
For each component, use its smallest compatible positive observed paired group,
with stable-ID tie resolution. For its national valid group shares:

$$
d_j^{SC}=\operatorname{clip}_{[-1,1]}\log(z_{Sj}/z_{Cj}),\quad
q_{Sj}\propto\widetilde q_{Cj}\exp(gd_j^{SC}),\quad
a_S=\operatorname{logistic}(\operatorname{logit}\widetilde a_C+gd_0^{SC}),
$$

where $d_0^{SC}$ is the paired non-valid log-odds gap clipped to $[-1,1]$.
$\widetilde q_C,\widetilde a_C$ are Camera-block posterior draws chosen for the
Senate path, not European coordinates. Camera/Senato row indices are chosen
using Gaussian-copula uniforms; they need not be identical. For Gaussian streams
and the declared profile settings,

$$
Z_S=\rho Z_C+\sqrt{1-\rho^2}\epsilon_S,\qquad \rho=v+(1-v)r.
$$

Local Senate profile means add paired residual gaps after subtracting the national
gap, clipped to $[-1,1]$ and weighted by
$\min(V_C,V_S)/[\min(V_C,V_S)+500]$. The mean adjustment is multiplied by $g$ at
use. Pooled groups receive a shared observed contrast. Missing contrasts use a
labelled zero gap and add 0.45 SD in quadrature to the inherited profile. Local
scales inherit Camera. No extra shock is added by the adapter; one paired election
does not estimate forecast-error correlation. Age-25+ Senate observations do not
stand in for the common-18+ contrast.

## 5. Unrestricted reference field and unidentified splits

From a country draw form $b=(a,(1-a)q_1,\ldots,(1-a)q_K)$, using declared fallback
parameters for new components. Before future filing, start with log scores

$$
\ell_{ij}=\log b_j+d_{c(i)j}+s_{c(i)j}\xi_{c(i)j},\qquad
\xi_{cj}=\sqrt{\rho_R}Z_{r(c)j}+\sqrt{1-\rho_R}Z_{cj}.
$$

Municipal noise is shared across its physical splits. Both terms use the chamber
coupling above; $\rho_R$ reallocates profile variance rather than adding it.
Multiplicative unrestricted raking calibrates the electorate-weighted field to
$b$. Country support is therefore an aggregate, not a raw intercept altered
unintentionally by geographic scores.

For an unidentified identical-column group $G$, use one group profile/noise and
separate internal contrasts:

$$
\ell_{ij}=\log b_G+\overline d_{iG}+\overline s_{iG}\xi_{iG}
+\log\frac{\exp(\log b_j+\sqrt{1+0.45^2}\,\zeta_{ij})}
{\sum_{k\in G}\exp(\log b_k+\sqrt{1+0.45^2}\,\zeta_{ik})},\qquad j\in G.
$$

The bars are group-member averages of prepared means/SDs; $\xi_{iG}$ is one shared
noise coordinate. The provisional missing-history contrasts $\zeta$ have separate
municipal streams, not another independent copy of the group-profile error.
The factory reuses the regional component field for group and contrast queries;
these errors are therefore not claimed to be independent when $\rho_R>0$.
Remaining unidentified overlapping directions are projected off these internal subspaces,
orthonormalized, then applied as separate $\sqrt{1+0.45^2}\,v_dZ_d$ log contrasts.
No historical group counts are split. Available separate local/regional evidence
continues to determine identifiable component profiles.

At a scope explicitly referencing every member of $G$, component references
can intervene on $b_G$: first calibrate the current group total, then calibrate
within-group composition while holding each cell's group mass fixed. For target
component totals $r_j$:

$$
p^*_{ij}=p^*_{iG}\frac{w_{ij}e^{\alpha_j}}{\sum_{k\in G}w_{ik}e^{\alpha_k}},\qquad
\sum_{j\in G}p^*_{ij}=p^*_{iG}.
$$

Choose $\alpha$ to match reference-date valid-weighted aggregates. If no geography
distinguishes A/B and their targets are 10%/20%, the central split is 1:2 across
the group field. This is an assumption supplied by references, not evidence of
historical component counts. New components require explicit references and
fallback profile/innovation scales; missing mandatory references fail.

## 6. Component-reference uncertainty and scope

Unlike M01, M02 uses scenario-reference SDs. At a scope/channel, eliminate exact
reference mass $F$. For uncertain components plus an optional free residual,

$$
Z_g=\mu_g+s_g\epsilon_g,\quad\epsilon_g\overset{ind}{\sim}N(0,1),\qquad
R_g=(1-F)\frac{e^{Z_g}}{\sum_he^{Z_h}}.
$$

Calibrate **after** this joint transform to $E[R_g]=m_g$ and
$\operatorname{SD}(R_g)=d_g/100$. With a free residual fix its score to zero;
otherwise remove additive-score non-identifiability. The calibrated diagonal-score
solution minimizes $\sum s_g^2$, then $\sum\mu_g^2$, with stable tie handling.
Mean/SD checks require $10^{-6}$ accuracy and integration refinement below
$10^{-7}$. Incompatible moments fail; no clipping, per-draw repair or retries.
Boundary means require zero SD. Fully specified complementary pairs have equal
SDs. A fully specified positive central total is normalized once, including SDs,
with an audit warning; partial overfull inputs and zero totals fail.

For a political intervention with current per-cell non-valid support $a_i$,
rake the supported component/group scores $b_{ij}$ using valid-vote weights:

$$
p'_{ij}=(1-a_i)\frac{b_{ij}e^{\lambda_j}}{\sum_{k>0}b_{ik}e^{\lambda_k}},\qquad
\frac{\sum_i E_i p'_{ij}}{\sum_i E_i(1-a_i)}=R_j.
$$

The free residual preserves its within-cell proportions. Positive unsupported
targets require declared new/missing support policy, not arbitrary epsilon.
A partially referenced unidentified group uses this ordinary component raking;
the fixed-group-mass split above applies to scopes with all its member targets.
For a non-valid target, instead use elector weights and retain political ratios:
interior solutions have $a'_i=\operatorname{logistic}(\operatorname{logit}a_i+\lambda)$
and $\sum_iE_i a'_i/\sum_iE_i=R_0$. Explicit non-valid interventions permit endpoint
support with labelled 0.5 starting weights, as in the shared implementation.

For a sampled common political anchor $R$ and compatible central chamber shares
$b_C,b_S$, use the declared gap sensitivity $g$:

$$
\delta=\tfrac12g\operatorname{clip}_{[-1,1]}
 [\operatorname{logit}b_S-\operatorname{logit}b_C],\qquad
R_C=\operatorname{logistic}(\operatorname{logit}R-\delta/2),\quad
R_S=\operatorname{logistic}(\operatorname{logit}R+\delta/2).
$$

Endpoints stay exact; missing baseline contrasts have labelled fallbacks.
The common mean/SD are before this nonlinear map. One sampled common anchor
couples both chambers; an explicit chamber target replaces that chamber's value.
Resolve national → region → province → municipality. More-specific references
supersede the affected broader-scope targets and may change the resulting country
aggregate. Apply non-valid interventions before political ones at a scope.
Do not freeze all other components, reimpose a superseded national target, constrain
references by future filing capacity, or treat interventions as historical data
or fixed election-day margins.

## 7. Future movement: bounded mass-pair field

In each draw, movement scales are perturbed independently of reference fitting:

$$
\sigma^{(s)}=\operatorname{clip}_{[0.06,1]}(\widehat\sigma e^{0.2Z}),\qquad
\sigma_0^{(s)}=\operatorname{clip}_{[0.025,0.7]}(\widehat\sigma_0e^{0.2Z_0}).
$$

For fitted political components use $\sigma_j=n_j\sigma^{(s)}$; a new component
uses $n_j$ times its declared national innovation SD. $n_j$ is the first carrying
list's national multiplier in chamber scenario order, default 1. Cell-local
multipliers use the actually filed carrier, default 1; they do not remove
unfiled support. M01 ignores these M02-specific multipliers.

Use two phases: movement exposure $\Delta$ and political discrepancy exposure 1.
For positive exposure $d$, use $m=\lceil d/0.25\rceil$ steps of $dt=d/m$.
The discrepancy phase remains at zero calendar horizon and has no turnout or
local-innovation shock. There is no old Beta-stick forecast in this version.

At each step, group cells by the positive political support pattern of their
**reference** vectors, not future filing. Within each pattern compute current
component electorate mass $M_j=\sum_iE_ip_{ij}$. Draw exponential priorities
$e_j\sim\operatorname{Exp}(1)$, sort by $e_j/\max(10^{-300},M_j)$ and pair neighbours.
Priorities are independent of the step's new Gaussian shocks. An unmatched
political component stays unchanged during that pair update.

For movement, shock scores are

$$
S_{ij}=f\sqrt{dt}\left[\sigma_jZ_j+
\tau_j\lambda_{ij}\{\sqrt{0.15}Z_{r(i)j}+\sqrt{0.85}Z_{c(i)j}\}\right],
$$

where $f$ is the forecast scale multiplier, $\tau_j$ the future local SD and
$\lambda_{ij}$ the cell-local multiplier. Outcome 0 uses $\sigma_0^{(s)}$ for its
national term and its non-valid local scale. Streams use the chamber coupling
above; municipal noise is shared across physical splits.

For a political pair $j,k$:

$$
\delta_{ijk}=\frac{p_{ij}p_{ik}}{p_{ij}+p_{ik}}
\tanh\frac{S_{ij}-S_{ik}}{\sqrt2},\qquad
(p'_{ij},p'_{ik})=(p_{ij}+\delta_{ijk},p_{ik}-\delta_{ijk}).
$$

Skip a zero-total pair. The bound preserves nonnegativity and pair mass without
clipping. Conditional on the current field and matching, the symmetric Gaussian
difference gives zero expected transfer. Matching is state-dependent but
independent of the new shock; this conditional martingale argument still applies.

During movement only, non-valid support evolves separately and political mass
is rescaled together:

$$
a'_i=a_i+a_i(1-a_i)\tanh S_{i0},\qquad
p'_{ij}\leftarrow p'_{ij}\frac{1-a'_i}{1-a_i}\quad(j>0).
$$

For election-realization discrepancy replace $\sigma_j$ by
$1.5\widehat\eta n_j$, with no geographic term or non-valid update. Estimate
$\widehat\eta$ from training elections and their latest compatible same-kind poll
within 45 days, using common observable groups with shares at least 0.005:

$$
U_e=\operatorname{median}_{g<h}\max\left(0,
\frac{[\log(z_g/z_h)-\log(r_g/r_h)]^2}{2}
-\frac{1/r_g+1/r_h}{2n_e}-\widehat\sigma^2d_e-0.12^2\right),
$$

$$
\widehat\eta=\operatorname{clip}_{[0.10,0.50]}
\sqrt{\frac{4(0.25)^2+\sum U_e}{4+M_\eta}}.
$$

$d_e$ is the poll-to-election gap in years; the four regularization units are not
synthetic elections. This is a moment estimate, not an exact variance decomposition.
Bounded increments do not cap cumulative long-horizon changes. Random turnout,
valid-weighted aggregate ratios and later filing conditioning need not preserve
all country/college means even when local transfer expectations are preserved.

## 8. Physical ballots, defaults and audit

After both phases, condition once on each actual future ballot, including 0:

$$
\pi_{ij}=\frac{\mathbf1_{j\in F_i}p^T_{ij}}{\sum_{k\in F_i}p^T_{ik}},\quad
X_i\sim\operatorname{Multinomial}(E_i,\pi_i),\quad
V_{i\ell}=\sum_{j>0}A_{i\ell j}X_{ij}.
$$

The remaining mass must be positive. $A$ is binary component/list membership,
with one carrier per component per ballot. Counts already belong to destination
cells; sum them to colleges and derive the Camera TAA proportional view once.
Do not relocate municipal totals or restore pre-filing reference margins.
Supplied vote/preference overrides belong to shared legal assembly, not the fit.
Conditioning is deterministic, including for ceased/unavailable components;
unresolved identities are errors. Electorate conservation and filing zeros hold
in every draw, while realized counts need not equal reference percentages.

The current editable defaults set $f=1.5$, $\rho_R=0.25$ and new-component fallback
$(d,s,\tau,\sigma)=(0,0.45,0.25,0.35)$. These are scenario inputs, not hidden
universal constants. Matched 2022 common-18+ electorates are held fixed for both
future chambers as a conditional assumption. The six default anchors (IdV, Futuro Nazionale, PLD/LibDem, +Uno, Progetto Civico,
PSI/civici) use 256 deterministic M02 reference-only
draws before adding those anchors, recording their means, sample SDs, seed and
parent fit/profile IDs. They are model estimates, not independent observations.

Record M02/version, scenario/data/fit/profile/numerical/RNG/implementation IDs,
identification and fallback status, sampled references, dated reference support,
election-date pre-filing support, retained filing mass/amplification and realized
counts separately. Do not claim empirical calibration from convergence tests,
shared data, normal run completion or successful legal scrutiny.
