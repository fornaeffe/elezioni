# M03v0.0.1 Full hierarchical model

Let $P$ contain every stable political component needed to represent the
historical observations and the scenario ballot.

Let $c$ denote a municipality or, where its ballots differ, the
municipality–college part used for generation.

For $j\in\{0\}\cup P$, define latent log scores

$$
s_{j,c,t}=\eta_{j,t}+\delta_{j,c,t}.
$$

Outcome $0$ means abstention plus blank/invalid
voting.

## Elections ##

Let $L_{c,e}$ be the lists on the ballot of election $e$ in municipality $c$, and $A_{c,e}\subseteq P$ the
components components represented among that lists. The member sets
$G_{l,c,e}$ must form a partition:

$$
G_{l,c,e}\ne\varnothing,\qquad
\bigcup_{l\in L_{c,e}}G_{l,c,e}=A_{c,e},\qquad
G_{l,c,e}\cap G_{m,c,e}=\varnothing\quad(l\ne m).
$$

A component belongs to zero or one list on each local ballot. In an incidence
matrix $M_{l,j,c,e}$, membership entries are zero or one; available-component
columns sum to one.

Use $\pi$ for component probabilities and $p$ for list probabilities. For an
election at $t_e$, put

$$
Z_{c,e}=\exp(s_{0,c,t_e})
+\sum_{h\in A_{c,e}}\exp(s_{h,c,t_e}),
$$

$$
\pi_{j,c,e}=
\begin{cases}
\exp(s_{j,c,t_e})/Z_{c,e}, & j\in\{0\}\cup A_{c,e},\\
0, & j\in P\setminus A_{c,e}.
\end{cases}
$$

Aggregate into the actual ballot lists:

$$
p_{l,c,e}=\sum_{j\in G_{l,c,e}}\pi_{j,c,e},
\qquad p_{0,c,e}=\pi_{0,c,e},
\qquad p_{0,c,e}+\sum_{l\in L_{c,e}}p_{l,c,e}=1.
$$

Let $E_{c,t_{e}}$ be electors, $V_{c,e}$ valid attributed votes and $y_{l,c,e}$ the
observed list counts. After reconciling valid candidate-only attribution,

$$
V_{c,e}=\sum_{l\in L_{c,e}}y_{l,c,e},
\qquad y_{0,c,e}=E_{c,e}-V_{c,e},
$$

$$
\left(y_{0,c,e},(y_{l,c,e})_{l\in L_{c,e}}\right)
\mid E_{c,t_{e}},\boldsymbol p_{c,e}
\sim \operatorname{Multinomial}
\left(E_{c,t_{e}},\left(p_{0,c,e},(p_{l,c,e})_{l\in L_{c,e}}\right)\right).
$$

## Polls ##

Define $0^*$ as not responding to a poll, with its latent log score $\eta_{0^*,t}$.

Let $L_{e}$ the possible answers of the poll $e$, and $A_{e}\subseteq P$ be the
components represented in the answers. The member sets
$G_{l,e}$ must form a partition:

$$
G_{l,e}\ne\varnothing,\qquad
\bigcup_{l\in L_{e}}G_{l,e}=A_{e},\qquad
G_{l,e}\cap G_{m,e}=\varnothing\quad(l\ne m).
$$

A component belongs to zero or one list on each local ballot. In an incidence
matrix $M_{l,j,e}$, membership entries are zero or one; available-component
columns sum to one.

If the poll's answers include a "other" answer, that answer should include all components not represented by the rest of the answers.

Let $C$ the set of the physical cells in which the poll answers are collected.

Use $\pi$ for component probabilities and $p$ for list probabilities. For a poll at $t_e$, for each $c \in C$, put

$$
Z_{c,e}=\exp(s_{0,c,t_e})
+\sum_{h\in A_{c,e}}\exp(s_{h,c,t_e}),
$$

$$
\pi_{j,c,e}=
\begin{cases}
\exp(s_{j,c,t_e})/Z_{c,e}, & j\in\{0\}\cup A_{c,e},\\
0, & j\in P\setminus A_{c,e}.
\end{cases}
$$

Aggregate into the actual ballot lists:

$$
p_{l,c,e}=\sum_{j\in G_{l,c,e}}\pi_{j,c,e},
\qquad p_{0,c,e}=\pi_{0,c,e},
\qquad p_{0,c,e}+\sum_{l\in L_{c,e}}p_{l,c,e}=1.
$$

And then over all the poll surveyed area, for each $l \in L_{e}$:

$$
p^{poll}_{l,e}=\frac{\sum_{c\in C}E_{c,t_{e}}p_{l,c,e}}{\sum_{c\in C}E_{c,t_{e}}(1-p_{0,c,e})}
$$

The observed response shares vector $\bm{r}_e$ is then:

$$
\bm{r}_e \sim \text{Dirichlet}(k \bm{p}^{poll}_{e})
$$

where $k$ is a concentration factor (representing poll uncertainty) with an appropriate proper prior.

## Drift

For each interval $\Delta = t_2 - t_1$ between any two consecutive polls and/or elections, from reference date $t_0$ backwards:

$$
\eta_{j,t_{1}} \sim \mathcal{N}(\eta_{j,t_2},\sigma_\eta \sqrt{\Delta})
$$
$$
\delta_{j,c,t} \sim \mathcal{N}(\eta_{j,t_2},\sigma_\delta \sqrt{\Delta})
$$

where $\sigma_\eta$ and $\sigma_\delta$ are parameters governing the drift speed,
with proper priors.

The same equations could be used to estimate $\eta_{j,t}$ and $\delta_{j,c,t}$ for future elections.

### Reference values

Optional, per-list scenario-provided reference shares should be treated as a poll done at the reference date.

Reference shares sum must be lower than or equal to 1. If the sum is lower than 1, 
the remaining fraction should be assigned to an implicit "other" answer,
representing all the components not yet represented by lists with a reference share.

## Priors and fitting

Adequate, informative proper priors should be put on $k$, $\eta_{j,t}$, $\delta_{j,c,t}$, $\sigma_\eta$ and $\sigma_\delta$ to ensure convergence in case of sparse data.

If fitting is done using a Monte Carlo method, initial values should be chosen 
adequately in order to speed up convergence.
